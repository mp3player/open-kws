Connection and testing notes

Use system Chrome or Safari and allow microphone access. LAN HTTP pages cannot use the live microphone API; trusted HTTPS is required. Avoid embedded in-app browsers.

Enroll up to five takes per keyword and multiple keywords. Templates are isolated by model, frontend, and window length. Re-enroll after changing weights. Reusing a label appends samples; it does not train or modify the model.

No speech recognizer or true VAD is included. Low-energy windows are skipped; noise, conversation, and similar-sounding phrases may still trigger. Thresholds and consecutive hits do not guarantee a false-alarm rate.
